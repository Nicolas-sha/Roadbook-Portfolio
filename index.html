4L Trophy: More Than a Rally

In 2024, I took on one of the most challenging adventures of my life: the 4L Trophy. A 6,000 km humanitarian rally through the Moroccan desert, driving a Renault 4L packed with school supplies for children in need.

This experience taught me resilience, teamwork, and the power of purpose-driven projects. It shaped my approach to problem-solving — navigating uncertainty, adapting to challenges, and always keeping the mission in focus.
